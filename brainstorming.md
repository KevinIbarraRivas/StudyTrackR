## APP BRAINSTORMING:
1. **Study Session Timer** (Similar sorta to Tomato Timer)
1. **Grade/Assignment Tracker** - Tracking of your current assignments and when they are due, alongside this tracking of your grades per class. Allows you to calculate your current GPA and what grades you would need on upcoming assignments to ensure the grade of your choosing
1. **Student Budget Tracker** - Log income and spending and sort them into categories to better budget your money to ensure you spend your money in the correct way.
2. **Game Time Logger** - Log gaming sessions per game and platform. Allow users to set personal time limits and see in a visual and more appealing way your own habits to allow for better decisons if needed.
3. **Habit balance tracker** - Similarly to the previous but encompassing all of your daily habits allowing users to track what they do throughout the way to better allocate your time and set goals and such for better habits
4. **Expense Splitter** - Allow a easy way to split up costs without much hassle and then track these purchases and such per person and category, Allows you to see who owes you what and for what
5. **HomeWork Helper** - An app that allows you to log and time your homework sessions per assignment categorized by the class and subject. This would all then be stored and used to calculate how much time you should devote per night for homework by looking at what assignments you have due and how long in the past it took to complete work for that class.
6. **RateAnything!** - Experimental app which allows users to rate absolutely anything. This would be done by allowing users to take a picture of something like a bench or anything and then rate it. This would use geological data to mark it on a map and show it to other users. Then to rate things other people have rated you would need to be in the same location and take a picture of the same thing. It would be limited to certain cities and places to see how it could  work on a smaller scale.
7. **Health PAL** - App which allows you to track your calories and excercise habits in a single place to allow you to see if you are being consistant in your health and fitness goals. It would send you friendly reminderes about your goals and if you are on track with what you had set your goals to when setting up the app.


## EVALUATION ON TOP TWO IDEAS
**IDEA 1: Study Session Tracker**
-
* **Description** : An app where students can log their study sessions by subject and class, run a live timer and, review the aformentioned study sessions through different charts and streaks. The users can then set personal weekly goals for themselves and even recieve helpful notifications to ensure consistancy. Alongside this through analyzing their data can make assumptions for the future
* **Category** : Productivity / Education.
* **Mobile** : Mobile is very important for this app. This is due to the timer being able to run smoothly on the phone ensuring that students can track their sessions anywhere and easily. The push notifications on a device which most people always have with the allows users to stay consistant with the constant reminders. 
* **Story**  : The value in this app is the easy tracking of their own studying habits overtime to better see their own  actions towards self improvement. Seeing their own work visualized with the charts and streaks can help motivate students to continue with these health studying habits.
* **Market** : I believe that there is a large market for this app mostly  students from all ages who want to better their own studying habits. It provides large value for the people who need it since it is something which can help with many different aspects of their life since it allows for better scheduling as well.
* **Habit** : The app would be very habit inducing since they would open up the app whenever they sit down and study. Since they make more data for themselves whenever they study and and then consume that same data by seeing their streaks and their saved data in the app.
* **Scope** : V1 would allow users to log sessions, pick a subject, run a timer, and see their history. Then V2 would allow users to add weekly goals and see their data in chart. V3 would allow for notification reminders and streaks. V4 if getting to this point could possibly allow for more social aspects such as leaderboards for longterm stats.

**IDEA 2: Student Budget Tracker**
-
* **Description** : Personal finance app which is designed for students to log their income and expenses by their category. Set monthly limits, and see visual breakdowns of how they are spending their money.
* **Category** : Finance/Productivity
* **Mobile** : Mobile allows for logging these expenses from anywhere. It can be done right after buying something without delay. Then the push notifications on the phone would notify the users if they are approaching their own set spending limit. Could be integrated with the camera to be able to scan recipts to ease the entering of data
* **Story** : Since many students do not know where their money goes on a month to month basis. This application would allow them to see their own spending in a easy way to stop wondering where the money has gone.
* **Market** : College students would be a large audience for this since money is a consient thing to keep track of. It could be expanded to young adults since it is something which would be benefical to them as well.
* **Habit** : Since it would easy to log information and see their own entered data it would be something which would become a habit when making purchases. Then it would become something which would be checked towards the end of the month to properly see how money was spent.
* **Scope** : V1 would let users log their transactions by categories and set personal monthly budgets. V2 would allow visual chartts and spending summaries for the month. V3 would allow budget notifications to notify when you are closing in on your limits


## The Final Decision
**Chosen app : Study Session Tracker**
According to my evaluation the Study Session Tracker is the strongest idea across the 5 criteria previously mentioned. Since it is focused on mobile first, compelling story for the auidence it is trying to appeal to and a strong habit causing loop. Alongside these reasons it also has a well structured sprint path for future improvements.
